# Image spec

The one scene every mockup builds. The CEO has signed off the wide layout in `mockups/wide-wireframe.png`. The CEO has signed off the look in `mockups/cut-paper/`. The fonts are placeholders.

## Style: Lane Smith

The defining style is Lane Smith's picture-book painting, as seen in the photos in `docs/reference/`. Name Lane Smith in every agent brief. Image prompts carry the words under Prompt wording. No page, character, or composition from his books is copied, traced, or given to an image model.

Lane Smith traits to hit:

- **Palette:** aged parchment cream, dusty slate blue, brick red, mustard ochre, moss green, and a near-black teal. Colours are muted and a little dirty. Bright accents are small.
- **Paint grain:** oil paint sprayed, sponged, and dry-brushed. Every surface is speckled or mottled, with scratches and flecks where the paint has lifted.
- **Edges:** shapes look cut out with scissors, crisp but wobbly. Some small pieces keep a torn white paper border.
- **Lighting:** forms glow from inside, bright at the centre and falling to dark at the rim. Heavy vignettes. Hard black silhouettes against a lit ground.
- **Figures:** lumpy, simple bodies; heavy-lidded eyes with tiny pupils; thin spidery lines for whiskers, hair, and legs. Deadpan and a little grumpy.
- **Type:** blackletter display for names, letter-spaced and set off-kilter. Old-style serif for sentences, in mixed sizes. Ornamental brackets around a label.

## Prompt wording

Prompts stay short. Every prompt ends with:

> Stencil-sharp shapes, speckled oil glazes. Deadpan, slightly creepy picture-book art.

Words that hold the look:

- **The creature:** "huge simple body", "one huge half-closed yellow eye and one tiny one", "soft glow from inside".
- **Layering:** "the body is one cut-paper piece laid over five separate, very thick, uneven arms".
- **Dark ground:** "dark, mottled, speckled painted paper".
- **Scraps:** "torn scraps of speckled painted paper".

## Pieces

The scene is a stack of separate pieces:

- The background: painted whole.
- The sea floor: painted whole, with each clump of weed as its own piece.
- The whale's rib: painted whole.
- Five arms: cut from a painted sheet by outlines drawn in code.
- The body: the outline of a painted piece, filled from a painted sheet.
- Two eyes, each an eyeball under its own lid: cut from painted sheets.
- Three painted fish, repeated to make the swarm.

## Background

The sea floor, drawn from this passage in Mervyn Peake's *Titus Groan*:

> ...his world of wavering light. His world of pearls and tendrils and his breath at his breast. Born as a plunger into the deeps he is at one with every swarm of lime-green fish, with every coloured sponge. As he holds himself to the ocean's faery floor, one hand clasped to a bedded whale's rib, he is complete and infinite. Pulse, power and universe sway in his body. He is in love.

What the scene takes from it:

- Wavering light falling from above through dark water.
- Pearls scattered on the floor.
- Tendrils of weed.
- A swarm of lime-green fish.
- Coloured sponges.
- A whale's rib bedded in the faery floor.

## Creature

A rust-red kraken, lit from inside. Its body fills the upper-left third of the wide scene and runs off the page. It has two uneven eyes: one very large, one small. Each is a round yellow eyeball with a dark pupil, under a lid that slides down to blink. The eyes blink slowly and seldom, a fraction of a second apart, in no fixed order.

## Arms

Five thick arms spread from under the body toward the right and the bottom. Each tapers, and none curls. Each has its own thickness, length, and text size. Each arm carries one project as real text along its length: the name, underlined, which links to the live site, then the logline, then *(code)* in italics for a public repository. Every arm wriggles very slowly all the time, and stops while a pointer rests on it.

## Dwellers

Six to ten lime-green fish slide back and forth in one patch of water, turning round at each end.

## Owner's mark

One large whale's rib lies low in the sea floor at the lower right, its wide end buried in a mound. It rises in a shallow curve and leaves the scene at the right edge. Real text runs along it: the name in large type, the tag beneath it in italics, the bio in quotation marks, and two profile links.

## Sea floor

Painted mounds, angled and uneven. The weed sways very slowly, each clump at its own rate.
