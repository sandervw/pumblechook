# Motion

**UC-09 Make an arm wriggle.** Steps: in a desktop window, rest the mouse pointer on an arm for three seconds; move the pointer off the arm onto empty water. Expected: the arm moves while the pointer rests on it; the other arms do not wriggle; the arm comes to rest within three seconds of the pointer leaving.

**UC-10 Watch the eyes blink.** Steps: open the site and touch nothing for 30 seconds. Expected: the creature's eyes close and reopen at least once; at least one smaller animal's eyes do the same.

**UC-11 Turn motion off.** Steps: set the device to reduce motion; open the site; touch nothing for 30 seconds; rest the pointer on an arm. Expected: nothing in the scene moves, with or without the pointer on an arm; every name, logline, and link can still be read and followed.
