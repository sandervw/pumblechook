# Motion

**UC-09 Stop an arm's wriggle.** Steps: in a desktop window, watch the arms for ten seconds; rest the mouse pointer on an arm for three seconds; move the pointer off the arm onto empty water. Expected: every arm wriggles slowly on its own; the arm under the pointer comes to rest within three seconds while the other arms keep moving; the arm wriggles again after the pointer leaves.

**UC-10 Watch the eyes blink.** Steps: open the site and touch nothing for 30 seconds. Expected: the creature's eyes close and reopen at least once; at least one dweller's eyes do the same.

**UC-11 Turn motion off.** Steps: set the device to reduce motion; open the site; touch nothing for 30 seconds; rest the pointer on an arm, then on an eye. Expected: nothing in the scene moves, with or without the pointer on an arm or an eye; every name, logline, and link can still be read and followed.

**UC-16 Stare into an eye.** Steps: in a desktop window, rest the pointer on each of the creature's eyes for two seconds, then move it away. Expected: the eye opens wide, grows, and shudders while the pointer rests on it, and does not blink; it settles back after the pointer leaves.
